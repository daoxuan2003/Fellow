# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Fellow serves one private couple using a mobile PWA in everyday life. Either partner may open the app for a quick glance, record something personal, act on a shared task, or revisit a memory. Both people need the same trustworthy relationship state without losing ownership and privacy boundaries.

## Product Purpose

Fellow brings a couple's shared memories, moods, plans, health, deliveries, study progress, expenses, wishes, and personal details into one intimate space. Success means the home screen feels immediately personal, shows real current data, makes the next action obvious, and remains dependable when either partner changes shared state.

## Positioning

Fellow is a living two-person scrapbook whose everyday utilities remain real, private, and synchronized.

## Brand Personality

Youthful, intimate, and handcrafted. The interface should feel like a carefully assembled page made by two people for each other: bright and tender rather than vintage, decorative without becoming fake, and expressive without weakening clarity or trust.

## Anti-references

- Generic application dashboards made from interchangeable icon-heading-text cards.
- Flat line icons used as substitutes for tangible books, cosmetics, parcels, paper, clips, pins, photographs, or receipts.
- Brown, beige, or deliberately aged palettes that make the relationship feel nostalgic rather than young and current.
- Fake sample metrics, placeholder transactions, invented photographs, or decorative content that looks real but is not backed by product data.
- Responsive reflow that rearranges the approved single-screen home composition into a different layout.
- Rough hand-drawn SVG approximations of real objects and decorative effects that visibly read as generated scaffolding.

## Design Principles

1. Put the relationship before the utilities: names, connection, shared photograph, messages, and memories establish the page before the nine daily-life actions.
2. Make materials believable: use real photographs and production-quality object assets for image-native ingredients; reserve HTML, CSS, and SVG for structure, data, and interactions they can reproduce credibly.
3. Keep data honest: every status, count, photo, transaction, task, and wish comes from real state or an explicit useful empty state.
4. Preserve the approved composition: the 430×932 home artboard includes its safe areas and scales as one unit across phones instead of reflowing.
5. Keep decoration subordinate to trust: controls remain semantic, touch-safe, accessible, and responsive to loading, error, empty, and partner-update states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The mobile web experience provides at least 44×44px touch targets, preserves visible keyboard focus, does not rely on color alone, respects reduced-motion preferences, and maintains readable contrast over photographs and tinted materials. Safe-area insets, narrow phones, long real content, loading, empty, error, and partner-updated states are required design conditions.
